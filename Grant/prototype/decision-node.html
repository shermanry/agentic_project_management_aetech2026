Direction A

The statement

Learned from Throughline. A colored band marks it as a decision: a light tint while open, solid once locked, amber when it needs review. The body is one sentence in a serif face that says what was decided, then the reason. Options and evidence stay on the decision page.

The balance

This one is built around the evidence. Each option gets a small balance, with what's against it on the left and what's for it on the right, so you can see how the weight is leaning. The diamond is hollow while the decision is open and filled once decided.

Recommendation

A on the canvas, C on the decision page

Use the statement (A) as the node: it's the calmest, it reads from far away, and it matches the Record that clients see. Use the balance (C) inside the decision page's Compare layout, where people have room to weigh options. Take one idea from B: when a decision is open, show its options as small pills under the question, so the canvas still hints at the choice.
